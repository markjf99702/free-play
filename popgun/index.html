Popgun

A shooting gallery. Twenty corks, and tin targets running along the rails.

Tap a target to fire a cork at it. The cork takes a moment to get there, so aim a little ahead of the fast ones. Ducks are 10, rabbits 20, and the little stars up top 30. A golden duck is 100, and a cork in the bell wins three more corks. Five hits in a row are worth 50 more. A ticket for every 20 points.
