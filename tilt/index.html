Table

How to play
  • Flip: tap and hold the left or right half of the screen. On a keyboard, Z and / (or the Shift keys, or ← →).
  • Launch: hold the Launch button and let go. The longer you hold, the harder the shot. Keyboard: hold Space or ↓.
  • Nudge: swipe up, or press ↑ or Space with the ball in play. Too many nudges and the machine tilts: the flippers go dead, and that ball scores nothing more.
  • Match: at game over the backbox stops on a number from 00 to 90. If it's the last two digits of your score, that's 10 more tickets.
