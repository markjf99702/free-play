Highrise

A stacker. Build the tower a floor at a time, all the way to the top.

A row of lit windows slides along each floor: press Stop to set it down on the floor below. Windows hanging over the edge fall off, and the next row is only as wide as what stayed up. It speeds up as it climbs, with two windows at most from floor 6 and one from floor 11. Every floor is a ticket. At floor 11 you can take 25 more and stop, or keep going: the top floor pays the trophy and 100.

On a keyboard: Space (or ↓) stops the row.
