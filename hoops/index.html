Hoops

The basketball machine from the end of the row.

Flick a ball up the screen at the hoop to shoot: how hard you flick is how far it goes. Four balls roll back down to you, so keep them coming. A basket is 2 points. Twenty seconds in, the hoop starts to slide from side to side, and in the last ten seconds a basket is worth 3. The gold ball is worth double, and three in a row set the rim alight: a point more a basket until you miss. A ticket for every 4 points.
