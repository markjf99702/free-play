Clutch

A claw machine full of plush, on free play. Whatever you win goes on your shelf.

Hold the button and the claw slides across. Let go and it drops, grabs, and carries what it caught back to the chute. Catch one square in the middle; one caught by the edge tends to slip. Now and then there's a lucky star in there.

On a keyboard: hold Space (or →) to move the claw, and let go to drop it.
