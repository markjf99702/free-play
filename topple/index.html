Topple

A coin pusher. A ticket for every two coins you push over the edge.

Touch the glass to aim the chute, and let go to drop a coin onto the moving pusher. As it slides back, coins get scraped off onto the deck, and as it slides forward, it pushes the deck's coins towards the edge. A cup holds 20 coins. When the lights along the back stop on a pocket, drop one through it for a shower of coins. Gold coins are worth ten, and there's always a prize on there somewhere.

On a keyboard: ← and → move the chute, and Space drops a coin.
